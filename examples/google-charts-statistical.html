MAIDR + Google Charts: Statistical & Relational Readings

Each chart below is drawn by an ordinary Google Charts class, and what it means lives in the draw options or in the analysis — neither of which the adapter is handed. So each one names its reading with an explicit chartType, and the two threshold charts are handed their cutoffs. Click on a chart and use the arrow keys to explore it through audio, text, and braille.
