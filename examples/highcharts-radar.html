Highcharts Radar and Polar Area Charts

Two Highcharts polar charts made accessible by the MAIDR Highcharts adapter. Both are declared with ordinary line and column series and only chart.polar says which chart was drawn. Click on a chart and use left and right arrow keys to walk the spokes: a spoke's stereo position follows its angle rather than its index, so a sweep goes out and comes back.
