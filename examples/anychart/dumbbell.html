MAIDR + AnyChart Dumbbell Chart

Click on the chart and use the left and right arrow keys to walk the countries, and the up and down arrow keys to move between the two ends of a pair. The gap between them travels with both ends, so a reader hears what the row does without holding one number while navigating to the other. Press B for braille, T for text descriptions, and S to toggle sonification.

The two ends are announced as Low and High — AnyChart's own names for the fields the rows carry. A range series records only which value is the smaller, never what the pair is a comparison of, so it cannot say "1990" and "2019" here; a caller who has those names sets startLabel and endLabel on the emitted layer.
