MAIDR + AnyChart Radar Chart

Click on the chart and use the left and right arrow keys to walk the spokes. The sonification pans with the spoke's own angle rather than sweeping left to right: 12 o'clock is centred, 3 o'clock is hard right, 6 o'clock is centred again — so the circle sounds like a circle rather than like a row of bars. Press B for braille, T for text descriptions, and S to toggle sonification.

Highlighting follows the first series' markers, which AnyChart draws as circles. A radar with several series draws each one's markers with a different symbol, in a layer of its own, and there is no reliable way to tell them apart in the rendered SVG — so highlighting is switched off for the whole chart rather than covering the wrong series. Pass an explicit selectors entry to bindAnyChart to restore it.
