MAIDR + AnyChart Funnel Chart

Click on the chart and use the left and right arrow keys to move between stages — a funnel is a single row, so up and down are out of bounds. Each stage announces its count, what share of the population that entered reached it, and how much of the previous stage survived. The pitch carries the retention rather than the count: on a raw scale the worst stage in a funnel is routinely not the biggest fall, and a listener hearing absolute heights would hear the two the wrong way round. Press B for braille, T for text descriptions, and S to toggle sonification.

anychart.pyramid() is read the same way — AnyChart draws both from one class, and only which end tapers differs.
