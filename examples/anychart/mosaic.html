MAIDR + AnyChart Marimekko (Mosaic) Chart

Click on the chart and use the left and right arrow keys to move between classes, and the up and down arrow keys to move between the levels of a column and its total. Every cell announces its column's share of all passengers alongside its own value — a class of six and one of six hundred would otherwise read identically, and that difference is the whole reason a marimekko varies its widths. Press B for braille, T for text descriptions, and S to toggle sonification.

anychart.mekko() and anychart.barmekko() are read the same way; they differ in the paddings and the default scale, not in what the tiles mean.
