Highcharts Parallel Coordinates

A Highcharts parallel coordinates plot made accessible by the MAIDR Highcharts adapter. Every column is a different quantity, so each value is pitched against its OWN axis rather than against one range for the chart: arrowing along one car hears it high here and low there, and two cars whose pitches swap between adjacent axes are the crossing lines that mean negative correlation. Click on the chart and use the arrow keys.
