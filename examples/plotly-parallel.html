Plotly.js Parallel Coordinates

A plotly.js parcoords trace auto-detected by MAIDR. The columns here are not one scale — miles per gallon beside horsepower beside weight — so the pitch is scaled per axis rather than for the layer. Arrowing along one car hears it high here and low there; arrowing down a column hears the cars ranked on that variable; and two cars whose pitches swap between adjacent axes are the crossing lines that mean negative correlation. Plotly draws these lines to a canvas rather than to SVG, so the layer sonifies and reads out but has no visual highlight.
