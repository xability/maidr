Highcharts Manhattan Plot

A Highcharts Manhattan plot made accessible by the MAIDR Highcharts adapter. The chart is one ordinary scatter series per chromosome, which is how the alternating colours are drawn, but it is one cloud read through one genome-wide threshold — so the reading is declared with significancePlot: { type: 'manhattan' } and the adapter merges the series into a single layer. Each point announces its SNP and its chromosome, and the summary opens with how many clear the line. Click on the chart and use the arrow keys.
