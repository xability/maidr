Plotly.js Area Charts

Three filled plotly.js charts auto-detected by MAIDR. Click on a chart and use arrow keys to navigate; up and down move between series. On the stacked charts each point is announced with the running total of the column it sits in and its share of that total, which is the number the band heights are drawn to compare.

Filled bands, independent of one another (fill)

Bands stacked on one another (stackgroup)

Bands as shares of a common total (groupnorm)
