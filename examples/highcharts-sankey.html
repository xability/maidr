Highcharts Sankey Diagram

A Highcharts sankey diagram made accessible by the MAIDR Highcharts adapter. Click on the chart and use the arrow keys to walk the graph: Right follows the largest flow out of a node to the next stage, Left the largest flow into it from the stage before, and Up and Down move between the nodes stacked at the same stage. The nodes are derived from the links, so nothing has to be declared twice.
