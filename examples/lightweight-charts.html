MAIDR + TradingView Lightweight Charts

Sixty trading days of a fictional stock, drawn by Lightweight Charts on a canvas: candlesticks with a 10-day moving average in the top pane, and volume in the pane below. Click the chart (or Tab to it), then use the arrow keys. Left and Right move between days; Up and Down move through a candle's open, high, low and close. PageUp and PageDown switch between the candlesticks and the moving average; press Enter on the figure to choose the price or the volume pane. Toggle B for Braille, T for Text and S for Sonification.
