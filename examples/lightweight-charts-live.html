MAIDR + Lightweight Charts: Live Price Ticker

A simulated feed streams into a Lightweight Charts candlestick chart with a volume pane. Every tick revises the forming candle with series.update(); every fifth tick opens a new one. MAIDR follows the chart on its own: press M inside the price pane to turn on monitor mode, and each new candle is sonified and announced while you keep your place. Ctrl/Cmd + Right Arrow jumps to the newest candle.
